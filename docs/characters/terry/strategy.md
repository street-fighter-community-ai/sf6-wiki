# Terry Strategy

## Neutral

Terry has a variety of ways to approach neutral, and has strong tools overall:

1. 2MK. Terry has one of the best crouching medium kicks in the game. It is cancellable (i.e., 2MK &gt; DR~5LP leads to a guaranteed throw mixup), has very long range, and has the option of following up with 2MK~2HK target combo for risky yet meterless damage.

2. 5LK, 2MP, 5MP, 5MK, 5HK are some of Terry's best neutral buttons. You should experiment with the ranges of each button, and you will slowly develop an understanding of when to use them. For example, 5LK is an amazing drive rush check (i.e., 5LK&gt;214MP), and a great button to counterpoke with at closer ranges.

3. OD Power Wave. This is maybe Terry's strongest tool to "skip neutral". It's a very slow moving OD projectile, allowing you to drive rush behind it to get your offense started, and requires the opponent to double perfect parry to more effectively counterplay your offense. Be mindful of your resources however, as doing OD Power Wave -&gt; drive rush is very expensive at 3 bars. If the opponent gets hit by it, or jumps into it, you can get a followup combo, which will put you in a very strong position.

4. M Burn Knuckle. This is a classic "Dash punch" style of move, where it is punishable if used point blank, but can be safe, or even plus if properly spaced (There will be a slight screen shake when the move is plus on block). For Terry players, this move is kind of like the Green Goblin mask calling to you. While yes, this is a very strong tool to skip neutral, it can end up becoming a very bad and exploitable habit, and you might become overly reliant on it. If the opponent expects it and gets out of the way, or walks forward and blocks, etc., you will likely be punished. However, this is still a very strong move, and you should still use it to catch opponents who aren't expecting it.

5. Drive Rush. Terry has a good drive rush - not exceptional, but not bad either. Using drive rush into 5LP is very strong, potentially leading to a tick throw, or if you do a string like: DR~5LP, 2MP - this will frame trap, and on hit confirming into something like: DR~5LP, 2MP, 5LK&gt;236LK, 2LP&gt;214MP.
