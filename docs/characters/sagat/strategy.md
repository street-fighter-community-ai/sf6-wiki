# Sagat Strategy

## Neutral

Sagat has slow movement speed, but dominates at midrange with fast projectiles and long normals that can frustrate opponents into taking a riskier approach with jumps, anti-fireball moves, and Drive Impact.

5MK - A great all-purpose midrange poke with slightly less range against crouching opponents. Can be confirmed into a Target Combo for a knockdown, though the confirm window is relatively short. This button is very difficult to counterpoke since it doesn't extend a hurtbox before the active frames, but the whiff recovery can be punished effectively by mid-height normals. Much less susceptible to jumps and DI than 5HP, but can still lose if the opponent predicts your timing.

5HP - One of the longest non-Dhalsim buttons in the game, with a Target Combo extension that's fairly easy to hitconfirm. Very slow startup makes it impractical as a whiff punish tool, and the recovery is easily whiff punished by mid-height normals. Can be used like a midrange fireball that deals more Drive damage when blocked. This button is risky against opponents that like to jump or predict your pokes with DI, but if the opponent is trying to DI your poke on reaction, there's a good chance Sagat will recovery in time to punish.

2HP - Essentially Sagat's version of the common shoto 5HP with its range and full cancelability. It can be hitconfirmed directly into a special or Drive Rush, or buffered into a safe Low Tiger Shot. The long confirm window also makes it a much safer option against DI.

2MP - A great buffer tool and counterpoke comparable to Cammy or Marisa's 2MP. It doesn't reach its full range until frame 8, where it becomes one of the longest 2MPs in the game. In addition to special and DR cancels, it also has two Target Combo extensions: into ~HK for a Super cancel, or ~HP for a fully cancelable extension if you land a 2MP whiff punish.

5LK - Similar to Zangief's 5LK with 7f startup and long range making it more similar to a medium normal. Very unrewarding when canceled into Drive Rush, as it does not combo into anything naturally, and even on CH/PC it only gives a weak heavily scaled extension. In neutral, this is primarily used as a less committal buffer tool into HP Tiger Uppercut.

2MK - A long, disjointed low poke with faster startup than Sagat's other ranged tools. Not cancelable, and even after a whiff punish there are no real follow-up options outside close range. A solid tool to force the opponent to crouch block more often, allowing Sagat to get away with Tiger Knee pressure. Also much safer against jump-ins than his standing pokes due to the shorter recovery and crouching hurtbox.

6HK - In neutral, this forward-moving kick is risky to attempt since it whiffs on crouchers. However, opponents looking for Tiger Knee are likely to react with stand block or parry when they see Sagat move forward, making it more practical as an approach tool.

6LK - A long range low stepkick that can be confirmed into a light combo on Counter-hit. Very whiff punishable if misspaced, and susceptible to DI with its reactable startup. Sagat is left outside throw range when properly spaced on block, making it harder for the opponent to effectively take back their turn.

Low Tiger Shot - A fireball with terrible recovery, but necessary to stop the opponent from freely crouching under all projectiles on reaction. Useful as a safe buffer tool from spaced buttons like 5LK and 2HP. The OD version is good for fireball wars with its fast speed and startup.

High Tiger Shot - A much more rewarding fireball if you can condition the opponent to stand block or parry, especially with MP Tiger Shot's slower travel speed. Great at setting up fireball traps since Sagat recovers so quickly. The OD version is one of Sagat's best moves with its massive advantage and guaranteed juggle to SA1, but the slow startup makes it harder to throw out in midrange fireball wars.

Tiger Knee - A great approach tool that can hop over low pokes and gives guaranteed frame advantage against crouch block. If the opponent can react with stand block, the spacing is a bit more strict to maintain advantage. Can also hop over fireballs if you predict their timing. Slower versions are susceptible to DI on reaction, though a very late DI input will not always punish when absorbing the Knee.

Tiger Nexus - The HK version in particular is a great approach tool, as the hurtbox doesn't shift forward until right before the active frames. This allows Sagat to remain safe on block while easily confirming into a follow-up on hit. This is also an effective tactic out of Drive Rush, as it temporarily halts his forward movement to make counterpokes whiff.

## Offense

Light Chains - all chainable light normals (5LP, 2LP, 2LK) have very small gaps between each blocked hit

5MP - +2 on block but with moderate pushback keeping Sagat out of throw range. It's sometimes possible to frame trap into 5LP~5LP, but it requires a point blank crouching opponent. Inputting another 5MP trades vs. 4f lights, allowing a link into 5LK (for consistency) or 2MP (closer ranges, crouching opponent). Meaty timing can make this a true frame trap setting up a stronger Counter-hit combo.

6HK - +4 on block but outside of throw range and whiffs on crouching. Not very practical unless you know the opponent will be stand blocking or parrying. Can lead to a simple CH 5MP ranged confirm if the opponent hits a button.

DR~5MP - +6 on block and inside throw range. Can create a true blockstring into itself, then frame trap into a third 5MP which still sets up a trade combo. Delaying the second 5MP for a frame trap allows for a better Counter-hit combo into 4HP. You can also press 5HK (1st hit) as a frame trap which sets up massive advantage on a 4f trade, but if crouch blocked Sagat has to spend meter to remain safe.

DR~2MP - +3 on block inside throw range, but is only effective if it connects at the closest range due to its poor DR momentum. A frame trap into CH 5MP gives him his optimal 4HP link at close range or 2MP from farther away.

DR~2MK - +2 on block in throw range if used from close. Essentially the same as blocked 5MP pressure but with more consistent follow-ups since it leaves Sagat closer to the opponent. The trade combo into 4HP will still whiff against most standing opponents.

DR~6MP - +2 on block in throw range, but the opponent can walk back to escape the throw. The trade combo into 5MP usually gets a 2MP conversion due to the pushback.

DR~6LK - +1 on block at the very edge of throw range. Cannot get a true tick throw against 4f normals, and delaying the throw at all lets opponents walk out of range. 2LK can start a chain to punish back walk, but this also trades with 4f normals. Only 2LP can actually frame trap properly, but this doesn't help to achieve real pressure or prevent back walking.

2MP/2HP/5HK &gt; OD High Tiger Shot - frame traps with a 2-3f gap, and leaves Sagat +6 on block afterwards. In the corner you can microwalk for a throw or a real 5MP frame trap, while midscreen you can just pressure with a ranged button like 2MP or 2MK.

214K~6MK - +4 on block inside throw range, but whiffs on crouching opponents. Gives a strong strike/throw mixup if you can condition the opponent to stand block or Parry.

214K~6HK - +2 on block and allows the opponent to walk back out of throw range. The ~6HK follow-up also cannot be safely frame trapped into without Burnout.
