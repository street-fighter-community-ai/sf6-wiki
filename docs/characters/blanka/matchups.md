## E.Honda

A frustrating character whose core moveset counteracts many of your tools. Patient, grounded play and careful use of Parry is the key to navigating the matchup.

Blanka has superior range on his grounded pokes, so focus on this rather than trying a risky aerial approach that loses to Headbutt. Honda wants to chip at your Drive gauge with safe specials, so try to maintain a spacing where you can react with Parry while not getting baited by a whiffed LP Headbutt into Oicho Throw. Be ready to 5HK anti-air a random Sumo Smash to start a juggle.

Honda has no throw invulnerable options until he has SA1, so throw loops and shimmies are a good way to score easy damage. Once he has meter, treat him like any other character on wakeup.

If Honda's health is low, he cannot survive absorbing your attacks with OD Headbutt, especially heavier buttons. He also loses charge if you cross him up with moves like Backstep Roll or Surprise Hop.

Honda can shred Drive meter quickly, so it's important to Parry his moves more often than you block them. You can also try to interrupt incoming specials with pokes like 4MK, 2MP, or 5MK. After a Headbutt, Honda is safe and outside of throw range; you can take your turn back with 2LP, but this runs the risk of being Perfect Parried if done predictably. Sometimes it's a good idea to wait and react to the Parry with a walk up Throw. Honda may also try an immediate OD Headbutt as a gimmick, which can be punished severely.

## Guile

Standing at mid/far range where 3HP can punish Sonic Boom and 2MP can stuff Drive Rush approaches is a strong position for Blanka. This is also the range where Guiles like to throw LP Boom and chase with Drive Rush to throw off your Perfect Parry timing and get strong frame advantage. Staying in Slide range often causes Guile players to do one of two things:

Being closer than this in neutral tends to be more advantageous for Guile, as he has strong pokes and Sonic Boom becomes less reactable. 6HP can be used safely from just outside Blanka's medium poke ranges if you predict a Boom or a 2MK, though if you misjudge the spacing it's easily punished by HK Somersault. He can also punish at max range with SA1, but you can enter Coward Crouch to make this whiff over your head (be careful not to stand into the lingering projectile).

If the opponent controls space well and doesn't let you Slide, you'll have to be more patient and try to walk Guile to the corner with pokes. Blocking a Sonic Boom doesn't drain much Drive gauge, so play it slow and steady until you can get close.

Focus on chipping away at Guile's Drive meter to force him into Burnout or make it harder for him to threaten with reversals or OD Boom. Once he's burned out, mixing in cross-up Hop pressure makes it hard for him to escape as he lacks a non-charge reversal. He can still Flash Kick your Blanka Balls on reaction, so don't use them mindlessly to approach.

Guile lacks a significant low threat, requiring a CH/PC or Drive Rush to convert into anything. This makes it easier to walk back out of pressure at minimal risk. Staying crouch blocked prevents Guile from getting point blank frame advantage with 6HP. Many Guile players also like to use DR~6HP after a ranged LP Boom, expecting you to Parry; by staying crouched, you may cause them to whiff and get a free punish.

Guile's 5MP is neutral on block, so you can force trades with 5LK rather than letting him continue fake pressure (unless your health is too low to take the hit). DR into Bazooka Knee is also 0 on block at most ranges, so don't take a throw or medium pressure here either.
