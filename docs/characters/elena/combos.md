# Elena Combos

Click "Expand" for information about combo notation in SF6:

Elena thrives off of spacing and overloading the opponents mental stack. You're going to benefit the most from utilizing different oki timings in order to effectively keep your opponent in the blender after the first knockdown and eventually bait out a punish to close out the round. Aiming for the ideal knockdown advantages of +36, +39, and +42, these three knockdowns are key to setting up roll oki that is DI safe and forces the opponent to either reversal or perfect parry on wakeup. Most of Elena's combos take your opponent most, if not all, of the way to the corner where your mix gets significantly stronger due to being able to control a large amount of space in front of you and introduce high/low meaty options.

It's important to note that due to Elena's narrow damage differential (i.e. Her damage floor and ceiling are relatively close together when spending equal resources and are often indifferent to the ender) there is rarely an "optimal" combo unless you're able to kill.

I've included some of the more common knockdowns below that will give you the advantages mentioned above:

Once you get in the corner, you have room to get more creative with your enders and oki but the above are a great starting point regardless of screen position. It's important to note that for +36/+37 to have "real" roll oki that can't just be DI'd you have to spend on OD roll. However, the longer a match goes on and the more you introduce different options you can start to get away with fake roll oki options.
