# Arjun Moves

## Introduction

Falsely accused of killing his partner, Arjun is a former officer on the lam who will not stop until he uncovers the truth behind the murder. Before he picked up the badge, he dreamt of being an actor.

Arjun is a unique brawler/zoner with a flip on the traditional grappler style. Rather than making the opponent constantly guess in close quarters, Arjun hammers his opponents with multiple plus on block specials that stack grey life on block, making the opponent's attempt to fight back twice as risky. One wrong decision sacrifices all that accrued grey life, intimidating opponents into passive play. When on defense, Arjun's opponent will be constantly deciding whether aggression is worth the risk of a grey life cash-in.

Arjun has a variety of decent normals; 5HP boasts gigantic range and a target combo followup that allows strike/throw mix mid-screen or a SA3 cancel in the corner. If his opponent tries to retreat, Arjun can throw out 2MP, a safe on block (if well spaced) sweep similar to Dee Jay's 2MK. Once you've gotten in, 5LP is a plus on block jab that leaves Arjun in throw range, starting his offensive pressure. 2HK is a unique if slow sweep that can be special cancelled to remain safe if blocked. Lastly, 5HK can be used as a long range anti-air with a target combo followup.

Arm of Justice is a central column of Arjun's offense. It can be canceled into or sent freely in neutral as it is always safe, can be spaced to be plus on block, and does a decent amount of gray life on block. If they take to the skies, Cruel Shoulder is a powerful body projectile that anti airs and knocks down the opponent right next to Arjun on hit, setting up one of his most important special moves -- Divine Pillar. Arjun blows into the ground and creates a rock spire that hits low and stays on the screen on a timer. He can change its eruption timing and placement, setting up strike-throw oki situations at most ranges, especially in the corner.

When using Shoulder and passing through a placed Pillar, Arjun grabs the Pillar and turns it into Justice Beyond, a column that swings in a 360 degree arc with immense range. This follow up deals staggering amounts of grey life and Drive Gauge damage, pushes the opponent to full screen, catches jumping opponents even if they are behind Arjun, and claims a ton of ground. It comes out insanely fast, blows through Drive Impact, is safe on block, and can even catch late Pillar launches. The only true option for your opponent to respond with is to Parry, giving Arjun opportunities to feint a light Shoulder and get a sneaky PC throw, starting his pressure loop all over again. And best of all, landing it as a clean hit gives him a ground bounce follow up, allowing him to link into other specials and do staggering damage from one surprise special. All of these offensive options when combined with built grey life make Arjun terrifying to defend against for long.

When your opponent starts to Parry to protect their life bar, throw in a Ghastly Interrogation, a command grab that either applies grey life, or cashes out grey life Arjun has already applied, scaring the opponent into jumping or backdashing. Opponents trying to zone Arjun out will be forced to deal with Indomitable Defense, a unique counter that only activates on strikes and stays active against projectiles (making him effectively invincible to even multi-hit fireballs and supers like Shinku Hadoken). Punish them from this with Moral Breakdown, a special followup that deals immense damage on hit or gray damage on block, and can be linked into after landing Shoulder with Pillar for huge punishes. If you hold the advantage from afar, utilize the Breathing Ring, a projectile similar to Ed's one-two fireball, but can be angled to catch jumps and hold your opponent in place.

Arjun might be a battering ram of a character, but he suffers from numerous weaknesses, such as no reliable reversals, many subpar normals, limited cancelable buttons, and slow walk and Drive Rush speeds. Many of Arjun's normals are either slow or situational, and he lacks a cancelable 2MK/2MP to start offensive pressure. Enhanced Shoulder is a phenomenal offensive tool, but placing a Pillar is punishable without proper oki, and when placed, the opponent can body block it to make Shoulder a riskier play. Indomitable Defense is a good option, but is too slow to be a reversal and suffers from throws, limiting his wakeup options. While he has good health, his walk speed makes neutral tricky and chasing a careful opponent difficult. If Arjun has not pressured his opponent with specials, his command throw cannot deal normal damage, only a serviceable amount of grey life, minimizing its use as a punish tool.

Arjun is not to be underestimated. Though he has some weaknesses, he is an incredibly unorthodox and intimidating character to fight, and his potential lies in tricking his rival into eating his brutal offensive pressure, preparing for the big cashout. Leave your opponent breathless with the path of Yoga.
