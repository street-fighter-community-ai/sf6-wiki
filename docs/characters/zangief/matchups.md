# Zangief Matchups

## A.K.I.

While A.K.I. can struggle in pure footsies against Zangief, she has numerous zoning options keep him at bay. Gief's slow movement makes her fireball and poison pool relatively easy to set up, especially when backed with the threat of her Serpent Lash. Once Zangief is poisoned, A.K.I.'s improved conversions shift the risk/reward massively in her favor, demanding even more respect. Zangief must carefully manage his resources to avoid playing into A.K.I.'s win condition, while actively adapting to her variety of tricky setups and approaches.

### A.K.I.'s Safe Jumps

### Burnout Pressure

Burning out in this matchup should be avoided at all costs, as A.K.I.'s suffocating Burnout pressure is at its best against Zangief. Assuming she has enough health to survive a trade, Zangief's lack of a reversal SA1 can make it impossible to escape her 2LP &gt; 5MP &gt; Heel Strike loop, while his unusually slow SA2 is easily baited with jabs. Once you reach the corner without at least 2 bars of Super, a DI stun is guaranteed from 5HK or DR~5MP/2MP. If she tries to cancel from something weaker, be prepared to absorb it with 5[HP].

### Blanka's Safe Jumps

### Punishing Horizontal Blanka Ball

Light and Medium Blanka Balls are very negative on block and leave Blanka close to Zangief, allowing him to punish with 2HK for a safe jump. Alternatively, Zangief can burn meter for a more powerful Drive Rush punish.

Heavy Blanka Ball sends Blanka further out, requiring Zangief to contest it on the way in with Double Lariat or 2MP. Luckily, it also has slower startup, making it more consistently reactable.

### Cammy's Safe Jumps

### 5HP~HK Target Combo

When not in Burnout, there is always a 9-frame gap between the 2nd and 3rd hits of Cammy's 5HP~HK Target Combo regardless of whether she cancels into a Divekick. If you see her cancel 5HP, be ready to press 2MP to counter Cammy on the ground or in the air. If it hits grounded, Zangief can link into 3MP for a full combo, while an airborne hit causes a Forced Knockdown that can juggle into OD Borscht Dynamite for huge damage.

Beating Cammy's SA3 Startup: OD Lariat (PPP) hits Cammy out of the air (don't use your own SA3 cancel)

### Dhalsim's Safe Jumps

### Using Siberian Express

While niche in other matchups, the far version of Siberian Express has practical use against Dhalsim. Despite its slow startup, it can immediately transition into the grab if it absorbs one of Dhalsim's ranged limbs, dealing great damage. While it should only be used sparingly, the threat of this armored grab can make Dhalsim players hesitate or switch to a more projectile-based gameplan in some situations.

## Ed

Ed excels at controlling the neutral against Zangief using his variety of midrange zoning tools. His long-ranged cancelable pokes allow him to convert from outside Zangief's medium range, while MK Flicker and 5MK can bypass 5[HP]'s armor with their downward angle. Zangief's slow movement lets Ed use his fireball and charged Flicker more freely, letting him set up mixups with little risk. This puts Zangief in a tough position, forcing him to rely on risky reads to close the gap and capitalize on Ed's defensive weaknesses.

### E. Honda's Safe Jumps

### Beating Sumo Headbutt

While Zangief can utilize the universal counterplay to Sumo Headbutt, 5[HP] allows Zangief to armor through and punish Headbutt attempts. Note that you do take a chunk of recoverable health for doing so. Even if Honda stops short with a lower-strength Headbutt, Zangief can release the HP button early to whiff punish it. Honda is also discouraged from using OD Sumo Headbutt as a wakeup option, as meaty SPD will ignore the armor and get huge Punish Counter damage.

### Beating Sumo Smash

Zangief has some of the strongest tools in the game for dealing with Sumo Smash. Double Lariat's large hitbox and instant input allows Zangief to easily anti-air Sumo Smash without much thought. 2HP also sees one of its few practical uses, since it hits high enough to cause a Punish Counter spinout that can juggle into OD Borscht Dynamite. 5[HP] also gets the job done, absorbing the downward hit and getting excellent oki with the sliding knockdown. Since it's the same button you use for countering Headbutt, it's easier to keep your mind focused on other aspects of the match like countering Drive Rush or normal jumps.

### OD Amnesia Bombs

### Playing Around OD Amnesia

OD Amnesia (22KK) is a powerful counter, giving him a full (albeit scaled) punish on Zangief's throws or heavy normals. However, if it counters one of Zangief's light normals, Gief remains at frame advantage and may be able to enforce a mixup that avoids the exploding projectiles. If your health is low, watch out for the threat of immediate SA1 when JP recovers from the counter animation.

A countered 2LK leaves Zangief at +3, leading to an immediate mixup between 3MP, Spinebuster (3LPLK), or SPD. Both of the throw animations last long enough to avoid the projectile detonations, while 3MP has a long hitconfirm window for a cancel to SA2 that similarly avoids them.

Zangief can also do this from a countered 5LP (+6) or 2LP (+8), but the mixup must be manually delayed.

### Kimberly's Safe Jumps

### Punishing Teleports

Zangief's best button to check Kimberly's teleports is 5MP. Against her normal teleport, he can continue with the Target Combo for solid damage and a knockdown, though it can whiff if she performs the held Feint version at longer ranges. It will cause a Forced Knockdown against OD Teleport, which can be followed with an instant aerial OD Borscht Dynamite, or potentially SA1 on a high connect.

2MP works similarly, though it's a bit harder to time consistently against an OD teleport. The upside is that it can cancel into Lariat, SA2, or OD Lariat &gt; SA3, and if it catches Kimberly airborne it's guaranteed to set up a juggle to SA1 at any height.

If you are certain a grounded teleport is coming, LP or OD SPD are also good options, though they are even more risky to whiff against a Feint teleport.

## Zangief

The Zangief mirror is either incredibly stale or incredibly goofy with no in-between. Zangief struggles to approach his own midrange options, but those same midrange options lead into very little, resulting in a slow back-and-forth of failed jump-ins and stuffed dashes. However, Zangief's lack of reversals paired with his overwhelming mixup means that if either player gets in, they can blend the opponent alive.
