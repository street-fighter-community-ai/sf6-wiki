## Offense

TO-DO

After Backfist Combo, Bison can use an immediate Psycho Crusher to gain frame advantage on block. This is susceptible to Perfect Parry as well as any reversals with full or anti-air invincibility. It can also lose to Drive Impact if you use the wrong strength. In order to stay safe from DI, use these routes:

Be aware that the first route into LP Crusher oki can be backdashed midscreen by most of the cast. If there is any notable pushback before Backfist Combo (e.g. 2LK, 2LP, 5LK &gt; LP Backfist), then this route whiffs on everyone's backdash (except Marisa after LP Backfist, LP Crusher). If the opponent demonstrates that they are willing to backdash in this oki scenario, it may be worth using HP Crusher despite the risk of DI. Be aware that LP Backfist, LP Crusher allows Bison to recover on the last possible frame against &#123;&#123;clr|DR|DI}, and as such will lose if LP Crusher is not executed on time.

A special note of Frame Kills: When next to a grounded opponent 5LK will still cause pushback on Bison. This property is not shared by 5LP, 2LP, or 2Lk. Due to this 5LP is the preferred Frame Kill as it allows Bison to Shimmy the opponent, allowing for more options and ambiguity to his Okizeme.

Knockdown Advantage: All scenarios assume that opponent will Back Rise midscreen when possible, unless otherwise stated.
