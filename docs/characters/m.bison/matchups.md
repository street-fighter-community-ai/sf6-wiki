# M. Bison Matchups

## A.K.I.

214LP has very long recovery, making OD Psycho Crusher a viable reaction (though she may be able to avoid punishment with her own OD Slide). If she uses this fireball as an oki tool after her SA1, Bison has no real option besides block or parry. SA2 is too slow to punish A.K.I., SA3 allows her to OD Slide and punish, and OD Psycho Crusher gets stuffed in the corner or is too slow to reach if midscreen.

A.K.I.'s stance Heel Strike (2PP~K) is -3 on block, allowing you to push a strike/throw mixup afterward. Many players will try to Perfect Parry in this situation, so it's important to mix between buttons like 2LK, 2LP, 2MP, and Throw to force her to respect your options. If she frame traps into Heel Strike from a blocked 5HK, there is a gap that gives you some defensive options:

A.K.I.'s anti-air game is fairly strong against regular jumps. 2HK is consistent at mid/long range, while 5HK is good from a bit closer. 236HP hits right above her, and 236MP reaches surprisingly far to catch neutral jumps. Using Shadow Rise to alter the timing of your descent makes it much harder for her to consistently anti-air.

Her 2MK is plus on block, and +5 if done from Drive Rush, so don't get caught pressing buttons in her offensive strings.

Her light normals have some of the worst range of the entire cast, making it fairly easy to space Scissor Kicks.

When applying corner pressure, be aware of her j.2HP cross-up normal. It reaches downward quickly, giving you less time to prepare an anti-air in response.

## Juri

Scissor Kick can avoid Juri's grounded projectiles and lead to a Punish Counter, making it hard for her to throw them out in neutral. LK Scissor tends to fall back into the fireball unless used from close range, so MK Scissor is the most reliable for balancing speed and range. It's a bit harder to counter the OD projectile on reaction, as it travels faster and recovers much more quickly, but it's still doable.

Juri's 5MP is +2 on block, but has significant pushback. She cannot immediately throw afterward, as it requires Drive Rush momentum to keep her close for a mixup. Slightly delaying a 2LP on defense has a good chance of interrupting her pressure while safely blocking if she goes for a frame trap.

## Lily

### Condor Wind Attack or Stock

Lily has a canned mixup by canceling various Heavy pokes (especially 2HP, but also 4HP/6HP) into her Condor Wind (214P) attack or Windclad Stock charge. The most reliable counter to this is SA1/SA3 when you see Lily cancel into her wind-up animation; if she used 214LP, it's a true blockstring and nothing will come out, while the reversal interrupts 214MP/214HP and punishes the 214[LP] Stock.

Reversal Drive Impact works similarly, but has 2 glaring weaknesses: it will not punish 2HP &gt; 214[LP] Stock (allowing her to counter with her own DI), and if she has SA3 available, she can cancel her MP/HP attacks on reaction. This is a much more viable option if she cancels from 4HP or 6HP, as these are punished by DI if she builds a stock (though it only gives a Counter-hit with no crumple).

If you block the Condor Wind attack, it's possible to punish with 2MK at some spacings. A cancel from 2HP is the easiest for her to make safe (starting from about the edge of throw range). Canceling from 4HP is safe around the edge of Bison's 2LP range, while a canceled 6HP is only safe if she does it near max range. Be careful not to get baited with a spacing trap going for a punish.

## Sagat

The matchup is fairly straightforward. In neutral, Sagat wants to keep you between the ranges of his 5MK and 5HP, where he can stuff your long ranged pokes (or make them whiff). This is a good range to keep LK Scissor Kick safe, but if you are predictable he can bait this out with neutral jump, backdash, DI, or a 2MP counter-poke. It's also a difficult range for reacting to fireballs. If you can make him whiff a ranged poke, Bison's 2HK is a great whiff punish to catch his extended hurtboxes, allowing you to get in close for oki. Use your superior walkspeed to set this up while keeping an eye out for jumps or Drive Rush.

If Sagat throws out Tiger Knee in neutral, you can reduce his frame advantage by stand blocking. You can also go for a Perfect Parry, but he can intentionally bait this with a whiffed Knee and punish with Throw.

L Tiger Shot has very long recovery, making it fairly easy to punish with OD Psycho Crusher at mid to long range, or even an immediate SA2 (but be careful not to get baited by a fast-recovering High Tiger Shot, which will almost certainly be safe to a reaction SA2). If Sagat throws a High Shot at closer ranges, crouch to make it whiff and potentially punish with 2HK. Note that the early startup of 3HK Slide will not go under a High Tiger Shot, but it can punish from longer ranges especially against the slower-moving MP fireball.

If Sagat throws a L Tiger Shot after hitting you midscreen with Drive Reversal, you can punish this with Back Rise + OD Psycho Crusher. Other fireballs will either recover too quickly or stuff Bison on startup.

Tiger Nexus is a Jinrai-type attack with three follow-ups:

Both the M/H follow-ups can be interrupted with 2LP, which also punishes the initial L/M Nexus, but this is likely to get Counter-hit often. Stand blocking is safer, but lets Sagat shred your Drive meter with the other follow-ups, while Parry leaves you susceptible to empty Tiger Nexus into Throw (and leaves Sagat at frame advantage if you block the M/H enders). Importantly, you cannot punish the L/M follow-ups with Drive Impact after you've already blocked the initial hit.

Tiger Uppercut is a fairly reliable anti-air like most DPs. You can often bait it by using OD Shadow Rise into OD Stomp right above his head; by varying the timing of your descent, it can be hard for him to avoid or anti-air this mixup.

Sagat does not have a natural throw loop; he must use Drive Rush to create one. He can safely bait your reversals by whiffing a light normal or delaying his button press (though technically you can punish this with a wakeup light normal if you know it's coming). Your best defensive option is usually to crouch block and input either a delayed throw tech or a delayed 2LP.
