# Luke Combos

This can be due to patches to the game, or developments in the metagame. You can help by updating it.

Click "Expand" for information about combo notation in SF6:

Luke's Charged Flash Knuckles have a regular and a Perfect version. The regular versions will be notated as 214[LP], 214[MP], 214[HP], and Perfect versions as pf.214LP, pf.214MP, pf.214HP.

Cancel your Light and Medium normals into Light/OD Knuckle, or into a Drive Rush. For a harder cancel that gives more meterless damage, cancel into 623HP at close range (the distance of a 2-hit light string).

Cancel 2HP into Medium Perfect Knuckle midscreen or Light Perfect/Charged Knuckle anywhere.
