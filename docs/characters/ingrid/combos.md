# Ingrid Combos

Click "Expand" for information about combo notation in SF6:

Note: When a move consumes Sun Crest stocks, it will be denoted with parentheses displaying the number of stocks consumed e.g. 214PP(2) or SA1(1). Moves are assumed to spend zero stocks unless otherwise noted.Super Art 2 strength will be denoted with a preceding L./M./H. to show which punch button is pressed. For example, M.SA2(2) will be used to show the medium punch version of super art 2 held to use two sun crest stocks.When a combo gains sun crest stocks, the value will be shown with the net change of stocks in parentheses.
