### Kimberly's Safe Jumps

Countering 6HK~Hop:
Press 5LP immediately after blocking 6HK; this will take back your turn if Kimberly does nothing, whiff safely is she hops back, or punish the Neutral or Forward hop. This causes a Forced Knockdown, allowing for a juggle into 6HP, 2HK, delayed SA3, or SA1(HP) + OD Flash Kick. Guile can also forward dash to sacrifice damage for a corner escape.

There are stronger juggle options if you know your opponent tends to choose a specific hop cancel. 2HP beats Neutral hop and gives a juggle into delayed HK Flash Kick, while 4MK beads a cross-up hop attempt and gives the same juggle. For a less risky option, jump back and react to the hop animation with Air Throw, which will catch both Forward/Neutral hops.

Be aware that Kimberly can Perfect Parry your 5LP if you do it every time, and she is +1 oB if starting with Drive Rush or Burnout.

Countering Teleport:
5HP is a consistent answer to both ground and aerial teleports. Grounded teleports will eat a Punish Counter combo if she is hit after reappearing, though it's only a Counter-hit if she's able to start her normal attack. Aerial teleports will get hit out of the air for a Forced Knockdown, allowing Guile to juggle into moves like 6MK, 2HK, or HK Flash Kick.

Other buttons like 4HP and 2HP allow Guile to maintain charge before the punish, but are more difficult to use as a counter.

Be careful if Kimberly teleports from farther away, as she can feint with a held button and cause 5HP to whiff.
