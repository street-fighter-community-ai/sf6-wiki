# Alex Strategy

## Neutral

Alex has a very unique toolkit and an arsenal of techniques to approach neutral.

## Offense

Light Chains - Any string of 3 chainable lights (5LP/2LP/2LK) will frame trap the opponent's non-invincible attacks.

Air Stampede - Can frame trap into any jab

Heavy Lariat - From an uncharged Lariat you can frame trap with 5LP, charged you can use 5MP

Slash elbow - From a spaced or meaty Elbow you can frame trap into 5LP

Oblique Stomp (4MK) - Can frame trap into 5LP

TO-DO

Knockdown Advantage: All scenarios assume that opponent will Back Rise midscreen when possible, unless otherwise stated.

Alex has a powerful true blockstring sequence that deals strong chip damage and if it frame traps can lead to a Backturn combo.

Flash Chop becomes +6 on block guaranteeing a 5MP which will then loop until you are out of range to which you can DR &gt; 2HP &gt; Flash Chop and start the loop again.
