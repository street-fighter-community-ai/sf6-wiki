## Guile

Using 214HK/214KK can force Guile to lose his down charge temporarily, though if he already has charge he can react with OD Somersault Kick. If he tries to react with a late meterless version, Manon will win since she's not actually airborne during the active frames.

Navigating Guile's fireball game is difficult, as the slow 236PP is rarely practical on reaction and very unsafe if blocked. Your best bet is to slowly inch forward trying to Perfect Parry incoming projectiles, and use the occasional jump at unexpected timings.

## Juri

In neutral, Juri's fireball can be countered somewhat reliably with 236PP on reaction, or even meterless versions if close enough. At long range (around 2/3 screen), you can also react with 214MK for a meterless counter, though at closer ranges this will run into the projectile. The 214HK/KK overheads do not generally work in this scenario, usually getting hit during recovery even in the best case.
